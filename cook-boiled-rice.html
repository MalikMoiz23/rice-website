Why this rice

How it is made

Step 01

Rinse

Two or three changes of water, until it stops going cloudy.

Step 02

Boil

Plenty of salted water, hard boil, no lid. Test a grain at eight minutes.

Step 03

Drain

Drain the moment it is done. Rice left sitting in hot water keeps cooking.

Step 04

Fluff

A fork, not a spoon, and only once. Then the lid back on for five minutes.
