Laminated PP, double-stitched mouth, printed lot number and mill date on every sack. Drag the bag to turn it. Switch the weight to see the two sizes side by side.

  • Laminated woven PP — moisture barrier, rodent-resistant weave.

03 — Process

Paddy in, sacks out

Six stages, all of them under our own roof. Nothing is toll-milled and nothing is bought finished and re-bagged.

Book a mill visit
  1. 01

    Procurement

    Paddy lifted from Sheikhupura and Hafizabad mandis through buyers we have used for twenty years. Every lot moisture-tested on arrival.

  2. 02

    Drying

    Brought down to 13% in an LSU dryer, slowly. Rush the drying and the grain fractures in the sheller — that is where broken percentage is won or lost.

  3. 03

    Ageing

    Twelve to twenty-four months in ventilated bins, still in the husk. Starch firms up, moisture evens out, aroma deepens.

  4. 04

    Shelling & polish

    Rubber-roll shellers rather than disc — gentler on a long grain. Two silky polish passes, water-assisted on the second.

  5. 05

    Grading & sortex

    Length grader pulls the brokens, then a colour sorter takes out chalky, discoloured and foreign grain at two passes.

  6. 06

    Sacking

    Weighed to net 25 or 50 kg, chain-stitched, lot-stamped and stacked. Loaded on the same day the order is confirmed where stock allows.
